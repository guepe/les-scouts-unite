# Surface brief: page d'accueil (front-page)

Scope: the WordPress front page of the Les Scouts – Unité theme (`les-scouts-unite/templates/front-page.html` and its patterns). Visitor mode: **Persuade** (a new family decides to join) while serving returning parents who come for the next meeting.

Audience and job: parents on a phone checking the next meeting; new families checking ages and how to register. Proof/content: the next meetings per section, the next unit event, registration status. All dates and places are synthetic placeholders the staff replaces in the editor.

Memorable moment: the federation blue as the notice board of the local, with real notices pinned on it.

Unresolved: real dates, places, staff contacts, registration link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the notice board at the door of the local: the federation's bleu foncé, pricked with the white topping, carries pinned paper notices that say what happens next. It refuses the hero-headline-over-three-equal-cards arrangement of association sites.

OWN-WORLD: Ground bleu foncé #0A4275 with the real white Fédération topping tiled at low opacity; notices are white paper (and one vert base #95C11F notice) with a soft offset shadow, a coloured push-pin, a slight rotation each; Caveat Brush handwritten labels in orange #EF7B00 / rouge #D51317; Muli 900 headlines tight; section colour swatches (Baladins #00A0DE, Louveteaux #296F52, Éclaireurs #004F9F, Pionniers #D51317) mark every row; thin vert foncé federation bar above a white header.

STORY: The visitor sees the unit name, then this week's meetings for every section, then the next unit event and that registration is open; they tap S'inscrire or a section, or scroll to news, sections and practical info.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Federation bar + white header (logo, unit name, menu). Board fills the rest: "site title (unit name)" in Muli 900 ~84px top-left with a Caveat Brush aside; below, a two-column grid: left (≈55%) the large "Prochaines réunions" notice with four section rows (swatch, section, date · lieu, horaire); right a stacked event notice and the green "Inscriptions 2026–2027" notice carrying the primary S'inscrire button.

FORM: the notice board of the local, position 4 on my ordered list of 7, dealt by the roll and chosen by the user; seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: notices are pinned on load one after another (small drop and settle) and straighten and lift on hover/focus; reduced motion shows them settled.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
